## Accessing the Platform

To ensure data security, access to the CELC platform is managed centrally through our Main Portal.

### Steps to Log In

1. Go to the main portal login page.
2. Enter your **institutional email** and assigned password.
3. Click the **"Log In"** button.

![Login Screen](/images/docs/login-screen.jpg)

## Shared Session and Subdomains

Once logged into the portal, your authentication is automatically maintained across this documentation site and other school services.

> **💡 Tip:** If your session expires, you will be automatically redirected to the portal to renew your credentials.

---

## Access Levels by Role

The documentation and features available to you will depend on your assigned role:

- **Students / Users:** Access to general usage guides and platform manuals.
- **Teachers:** Access to academic management guides and reports.
- **Administrators:** Full access to system architecture, backend, and network configurations.
